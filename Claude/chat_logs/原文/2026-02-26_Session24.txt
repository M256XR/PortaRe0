## 10. Potentially Problematic Connections / Design Issues

### CRITICAL Issues

**1. VL812 Pin 20 (VCC12I) Connected to GNDPWR**
- VCC12I is the input capacitor node for the VL812's internal 1.2V boost regulator (connected between VCC12I and GND, with LX as the switching node).
- It should connect to a **bypass capacitor to GND** (correct) — but my analysis shows VCC12I is labeled GNDPWR. This is almost certainly a misidentification in the automated trace: VCC12I (pin 20) is at coordinate (113.03, 195.58) and the GNDPWR symbol is at a nearby position. **Verify manually** whether VCC12I truly goes to ground (which would be wrong) or whether there's a decoupling cap between VCC12I and GND making it appear as GND in the graph.
